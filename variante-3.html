Strategic Thesis

Unpacking our investment thesis.

Direct Team & League Ownership

Formation-stage entry at the optimal valuation window.

We target emerging sports properties where institutional capital has not yet inflated valuations. Shaping operating agreements and media rights from inception delivers asymmetric governance leverage.

  • Direct ownership in emerging teams and leagues
  • Formation-stage entry with favorable governance rights
  • Active commercial sponsorship & distribution design
  • Structured downside protection with clear counterparty alignment
Discuss an Asset

Specialist Access & Co-Investment

Sport as entry point; enterprise markets as destination.

We invest alongside tier-one sports technology venture managers and participate in selective direct co-investments in companies capable of expanding beyond athletics into enterprise data, consumer health, and infrastructure.

  • Partnerships with specialist sports technology managers
  • Co-investment rights in high-conviction growth rounds
  • Enterprise scalability with commercial validation in live sport
  • Access to Justin’s network of team buyers and league operators
Explore Co-Investments

Founder-Led Capital Discipline

Unconstrained by rigid 10-year fund lifecycles.

JPZ Ventures is built to back sports institutions with patience. When capital is aligned with the multi-year cycle required to build a franchise, owners make decisions for long-term equity appreciation rather than premature exits.

  • Full P&L discipline informed by 13 years of sports operations
  • Direct principal-to-principal discussions without committee layers
  • Flexible holding horizons tailored to sports asset liquidity
  • New York headquarters with national and international reach
