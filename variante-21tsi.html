Founder & Managing Partner

Justin founded JPZ Ventures in July 2026, bringing experience in asset transactions, commercial revenue, and technology purchasing inside a live sports operation.

He spent thirteen years at Zayat Stables, from 2007 to 2020, including nine years as Racing & Stallion Manager, from 2011 to 2020, with full P&L responsibility. He built sponsorship and partnership revenue around assets that owned no media rights and evaluated technology against real operating decisions.
